---
name: TERAS institutional positioning
description: Defines TERAS Academy's role relative to official competency testing, assessment, and certification.
---

TERAS Academy is a capability-development and test-preparation platform, not an official competency-testing, assessment, or certification institution.

**Why:** TERAS supports participants before formal competency testing through structured learning, workshops, technical guidance, practice, readiness checks, and evidence preparation delivered online and offline. Official competency decisions remain with the authorized external institution or assessor.

**How to apply:** Product copy and interfaces may describe preparation, learning progress, practice evidence, readiness indicators, gaps, remediation, and preparation for assessment. Do not claim that TERAS officially certifies participants, conducts formal competency testing, or issues authoritative competency determinations.

TERAS must evolve additively from its existing capability-readiness foundation; do not rebuild it as a separate LMS or replace the stable safety, readiness, Path A/B, event-history, or Passport architecture.

**Why:** The LMS is the core learning engine inside the Capability Development Platform, while the existing capability and readiness engine already provides the protected institutional foundation.

**How to apply:** Add Course, Lesson, Activity, learning records, and transition contracts in controlled phases. Preserve compatibility and prove each phase with regression gates before expanding roles or adding AI.
